# Privacy Policy for Second Rise

**Last updated: June 2, 2026**

## Overview

Second Rise ("we", "our", or "us") is a fitness and wellness app designed to provide personalized workout recommendations. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## Data We Collect

**Account data**
- Email address and password (hashed — we never store your plain-text password)

**Health & fitness data (only if you connect a device)**
- Sleep scores, HRV, resting heart rate, recovery scores, strain, steps, body temperature deviation
- This data comes from devices you explicitly connect: Oura Ring, WHOOP, Google Fit, Fitbit, or Apple Health

**App usage data**
- Daily check-ins (energy level, body map, pain flags)
- Workout history and session feedback
- Workout recommendations generated for you

## How We Use Your Data

- To generate personalized daily workout recommendations
- To track your progress and workout history
- To compare your actual activity against your plan at end of day
- We do **not** sell your data to third parties
- We do **not** use your data for advertising

## Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Railway (railway.app) infrastructure. Health data from wearables is stored only after you explicitly authorize the connection.

## Third-Party Services

- **Oura, WHOOP, Google Fit, Fitbit**: Connected only with your explicit authorization via OAuth. We store only the health metrics needed for recommendations.
- **Apple Health**: Imported only when you manually upload an export file.
- **Anthropic Claude API**: Used to generate workout recommendations. Your profile and check-in data is sent to generate recommendations — no data is retained by Anthropic beyond the request.
- **YouTube**: Workout videos are embedded from YouTube. YouTube's own privacy policy applies to video playback.

## Data Retention

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Wearable connections can be disconnected from your Profile page at any time.

## Children's Privacy

Second Rise is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
